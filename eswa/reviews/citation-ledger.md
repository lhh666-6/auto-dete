# Citation ledger

The anonymous manuscript cites 28 keys and `references.bib` defines exactly those 28 keys. There are no missing or unused entries. Twenty-two DOI records resolved through Crossref, two arXiv DOI records were checked on arXiv, and four non-DOI records were checked on official NeurIPS, PMLR, or W3C pages.

Known metadata hazards were explicitly rejected: the unrelated DOI `10.1109/ICCV48922.2021.00978` is not used for DocFormer; the unrelated DOI `10.1145/375663.375670` is not used for data provenance; and no journal DOI is fabricated for the Gao et al. RAG survey.

Tool limitation: Paperpile and `scholarly` were unavailable, so library membership could not be checked. See `../../reviews/bib-validate/2026-08-16.md`.
