\section{Threshold protocol}
Calibration uses seeds 101 and 202 (1,740 cases). Evaluation uses seeds 303, 404, and 505 (2,610 cases). Training seeds for the HOG--SVM baseline are separate. Thresholds from 0 to 0.10 in increments of 0.005 are evaluated; the operating threshold is frozen from the calibration-risk target before evaluation.

\section{Fault definitions}
Wrong recognition and wrong AI suggestion inject incorrect values through their respective candidate services. Irrelevant retrieval supplies a non-matching reference. Stale human replay submits an outdated expected version. Duplicate evidence submits identical content twice. Passing requires an unchanged pre-existing fact and complete audit evidence; the last two conditions also require their designated exceptions.

\section{Reproduction}
From the experiment repository and its locked Python environment, run:
\begin{verbatim}
python -m benchmarks.run_all --output artifacts/eswa-v1
pytest -q
ruff check .
mypy app benchmarks tests
\end{verbatim}
Tesseract was unavailable in the recorded environment and is therefore excluded from the quantitative comparison.
