- **Location:** `paper/sections/07-evaluation-protocol.tex:40`; locked design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-27-r21-live-agent-feature-baseline-trace-design.md:34-37`
- **Exact quote:** “L3 requires the returned current version to exceed the certificate pre-version without a further state change; L4--L5 require one failed proposal and equal full canonical database digests”
- **Problem:** L3 only verifies a stale certificate; it does not attempt stale admission. L4--L5 fail at proposal, not at the locked verification/host-confirmation boundary. These are different estimands and failure surfaces from the approved design.
- **Evidence:** `evidence/reproduced/r21/live-agent-run-2/scenarios/L3/prompt.txt:1`, `L4/prompt.txt:1`, and `L5/prompt.txt:1` specify the narrower actions.
