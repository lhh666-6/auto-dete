\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Auto-Decte identifies correction-aware authoritative-state admission as the software relation connecting an AI candidate, a human-authorized value, and an authoritative fact. It constructs a complete successor from an exact persisted candidate and a candidate-bound human authorization, while retaining proposal origin and allowing the committed value to differ from the machine proposal.

Under the declared observation model, five information classes distinguish candidate substitution, correction erasure, stale replay, partial successors, and source ambiguity. Paired histories give a conditional irredundancy argument; Full Alloy checks and ablations supply bounded sufficiency-like and sensitivity evidence for their encoded relations. \system{} then realizes the basis with immutable candidate and authorization records, explicit authorized values, commit-time freshness, one atomic record successor, total field-source copy-forward, and reverse-trace validation. Selected formal--concrete projections and the 35-case catalogue connect the abstraction to persisted behavior.

The repeated live-agent benchmark adds the system-level implication. Across three qualified configurations, three prompt variants, fourteen scenarios, and 1,260 planned executions, agent-mediated behavior varied descriptively. Benign task completion was 320/335 behavior-evaluable runs (320/360 planned benign cells), and 93 runtime failures were retained with endpoint-specific evaluability. No unauthorized authoritative mutation occurred in 720 fixed invalid-tuple admission calls or 179 capability-unavailable branches. Within these executed host checks, behavioral variation did not displace the host-side admission contract as the authority boundary; the benchmark does not measure model-generated attack success or a population vulnerability rate.

The resulting contribution is a dual-value, field-level abstraction and characterization of the distinctions required to turn an AI proposal into an attributable complete authoritative successor. Authorization, freshness, provenance, and atomic commit remain component mechanisms; authoritative-state admission defines how they jointly preserve the transition from proposal to durable fact. Broader database engines, authenticated reviewer studies, operational workloads, public artifact reproduction, and new failure families remain future work.
