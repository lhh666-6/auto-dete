\section{Discussion and threats to validity}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What the evidence supports}

The principal result is a layered argument about correction-aware authoritative-state admission. The history and observation model identifies five distinctions; the Alloy model encodes them; ablations expose bounded malformed witnesses when selected encodings are removed; formal--concrete projection binds selected persisted executions to the same relation; and the concrete catalogue checks fail-closed behavior, complete successors, and trace outcomes. The lifecycle makes the dual-value case visible: the machine candidate remains 100 while the human-authorized and committed value is 101.

Each layer licenses a different inference. The paired-history argument explains why a class matters under the declared failure model. Alloy supplies finite sensitivity and separation evidence. Projection checks selected model--database correspondences. The catalogue, stateful tests, and concurrency tests exercise one implementation. The repeated benchmark tests whether agent behavior reaches authority through a restricted surface. Their composition is stronger than a mechanism list, but no layer substitutes for the others or proves universal correctness.

\subsection{Design implications}

First, authorized mutation and authoritative-state admission should be treated as different software boundaries. A mutation protocol may faithfully change an already authoritative object without representing how a previously non-authoritative proposal and a human decision jointly produced an authoritative value. Systems needing Correction must preserve both $x_c$ and $x_a$ and must not attribute the latter to the model.

Second, the five classes are semantic requirements rather than prescribed columns. A stable capability, content-addressed certificate, or database key may implement candidate identity; a version, state digest, or epoch may implement freshness. What matters is whether the mechanism can distinguish the paired histories in \cref{tab:distinctions}. This framing makes the characterization portable while keeping its scope explicit.

Third, successor completeness and source attribution are separate. Atomicity prevents a declared batch from producing only a subset of effects. A total source relation then distinguishes complete lineage from a value-identical snapshot with missing or conflicting origins. Treating both as ordinary ``transaction support'' loses that distinction.

Finally, the repeated benchmark illustrates behavioral variation without granting behavior authority. Completion, context recognition, and recovery varied across the qualified configurations, whereas the host-side challenge relation recorded no unauthorized authoritative mutation in 899 evaluable runs. The relevant architecture principle is not that agents will comply, but that authoritative change remains mediated by a separately checked contract.

\subsection{Formal and construct validity}

SAT and UNSAT describe finite Alloy commands under the stated scopes. The full checks support bounded separation for the encoded catalogue, and conjunct-removal witnesses show sensitivity to selected relational encodings. They do not prove global minimality, unique representation, or unbounded sufficiency. Some high-level classes map to multiple low-level conjuncts; conversely, the source-attribution class is exercised primarily through trace attacks rather than the eleven admission ablations.

The projection contains nine intended instances and twenty mutants, not a proof over all database states. The concrete no-op rule narrows one formal behavior, while SQLite uniqueness excludes one formally representable duplicate-source state. Residual shared-code defects may affect runner, normalizer, model, oracle, or benchmark. Independent projection and database oracles reduce but do not eliminate that risk.

Reverse-trace completeness means that persisted values, sources, certificates, authorizations, and transitions agree under implemented checks. It does not establish evidence truth, candidate accuracy, or sound human judgment. Likewise, Benign Task Completion and Unauthorized Authoritative Mutation measure different constructs; utility failure is not recoded as an authority violation, and runtime failure is reported separately.

\subsection{Concrete and performance validity}

The production-facing catalogue is finite, and concurrency evidence covers the declared SQLite schedules rather than every interleaving or database engine. The trusted implementation includes canonical serialization, hashing, the admission service, the database adapter, configured transaction semantics, and the principal-policy source. Porting the contract requires a new adapter and conformance evidence for the target engine.

The historical admission comparator writes an empty source map and remains only a lower bound. The persistence-equivalent arm reproduces the full relational post-state through a generic delta materializer. Its latency gap combines excluded validation/planning work with implementation-path differences and is not a causal estimate for one check. The optimized trace and baseline use the same grid in separate executions; query-count reduction follows from the access shape, while latency ratios may still reflect caching, scheduling, checkpointing, and runtime noise.

The lifecycle uses synthetic/public input and a scripted principal. Usability, reviewer error, authentication workflows, other databases, operational workloads, deletion, redaction, retention expiry, and tombstones require separate study. Manual entry remains a compatibility path with a derived certificate and is outside the AI-derived candidate claim.

\subsection{Repeated-agent validity}

The Final benchmark covers three qualified configurations from two provider families, three prompt variants, fourteen scenarios, and ten repetitions per cell. It is broader than the earlier one-off probe but remains a finite convenience population. Configuration identifiers name requested model endpoints and reasoning settings, not immutable model weights. Provider revisions, service load, decoding behavior, and network conditions may change.

Ninety-three of 1,260 planned executions ended in runtime failure, unevenly distributed across configurations (\cref{tab:runtime-failure}). Pilot qualification is a pre-Final selection gate, not a predictor of final runtime stability: D1 had 0/28 pilot failures and 76/420 Final failures, while G1 and G2 remained at 4/420 and 13/420. All configurations are retained because the locked protocol forbids post-hoc removal after Final start; runtime classes and configuration-specific rates are reported separately instead. Primary endpoint denominators therefore include only construct-evaluable runs, and all raw terminal classes remain visible. The study was not designed or powered for provider ranking; descriptive differences must not be read as stable model traits. No unavailable-tool attempt was observed, so that measure does not itself demonstrate behavioral heterogeneity. The observed variation lies in completion, context recognition, stale recognition, and recovery.

Zero unauthorized mutation in 899 evaluable challenges yields a one-sided 95\% upper bound of 0.33\%, not proof of zero risk. The challenge set covers the frozen tool surface and host actions; it does not address a compromised host, forged identity provider, arbitrary database administrator, novel exploit classes, or model access to fact-admission capability. The result is authority invariance within the executed population, not general agent robustness, prompt-injection security, or AI safety.

\subsection{Lifecycle, reproducibility, and novelty validity}

The artifact retains immutable source, locked configurations, raw attempts, normalized inputs, generated tables, and hash-linked manifests. The repeated benchmark entered the manuscript only after a separate staging check verified the complete Final population and returned \code{READY\_FOR\_MANUSCRIPT\_INTEGRATION}. A stable public deposit and clean external execution remain necessary for durable availability and independent reproduction.

The novelty search, updated through 3 September 2026, treats public disclosure independently of venue status. The closest collisions are Continuity Kernel, LatticeMind, When Memory Becomes Authority, MutMem, MemTxn, Correct Is Not Governed, SuperLocalMemory 4.0, and the stale-constraint study \citep{he2026continuity,zhou2026latticemind,zhan2026authoritycollapse,saidi2026mutmem,cui2026memtxn,salas2026governed,bhardwaj2026superlocalmemory,nakayashiki2026staleconstraints}. Against these precedents, the paper contributes a dual-value, human-authorized record-field admission relation and a five-class failure-distinguishability characterization supported by bounded formal and concrete evidence. The novelty claim is search-bounded: transaction boundaries, governed admission, candidate/authority separation, freshness, atomic activation, and provenance-preserving mutation are established neighboring mechanisms, and later disclosures may further narrow the remaining boundary.
