## Diagnosis

The current manuscript already identifies the correct relation and same-value separator. Its opening is substantially clearer than the older JSS version. The remaining problem is the order and allocation of explanation: the abstract reports test layers before explaining the policy distinction; contribution bullets name deliverables rather than their research achievements; the introduction repeats numerical results and trust caveats; and Related Work leaves human approval and interactive correction almost unexamined. That last gap matters more than making individual sentences shorter.

The revision must preserve proposal/authorized-value separation, the exact-target policy, commit-time freshness, atomic complete successors, copied-forward source identity, all quantitative results, and Proposition 1's observation model. Stronger visibility is justified; a broader novelty claim is not.
