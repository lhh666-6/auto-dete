## Critical Issues

### C1 [P0] Locked evaluation specification is not implemented

- **Category:** Internal consistency / methodological rigor
- **Location:** `eswa/sections/01-introduction.tex:16`; `eswa/sections/05-experimental-protocol.tex:7-19`; `eswa/sections/06-results.tex:4-78`
- **Exact quote:** “We provide a deterministic evaluation package covering 4,350 synthetic image cases, calibration/evaluation seed separation, seven perturbation families, five fault injections, resilience checks, and 70 automated tests.”
- **Problem:** The locked design requires structured forms, OMR, QR/ArUco ablations, candidate-isolation bypass attempts, and attempted direct fact writes. The benchmark runner generates digit crops only; the fault artifact contains wrong recognition, wrong LLM suggestion, irrelevant retrieval, stale replay, and duplicate evidence. It never directly challenges the claimed machine-to-fact noninterference boundary.
- **Additional statistical concern:** The 2,610 evaluation rows are not 2,610 independent base observations. They arise from three seeds × three fonts × ten digits, reused across 29 perturbation/severity conditions. The i.i.d. Wilson interval therefore understates dependence, and the abstract's phrase “2,610 independent evaluation cases” is not justified.
- **Fix:** Run and report end-to-end form/OMR/QR/ArUco ablations plus explicit direct-fact bypass attempts; regenerate artifacts, results, and the claim ledger. Use independent base renders or cluster-aware uncertainty by seed--font--digit unit, and remove the unsupported independence wording.

## Most likely ESWA rejection reasons

1. **Insufficient empirical validation for an application journal:** the industrial-form framing rests on synthetic single-cell digits, not an end-to-end form study.
2. **Incremental or under-demonstrated novelty:** candidate staging, human approval, versioning, and provenance are established engineering patterns; five tests do not yet establish a distinct scientific advance.
3. **Statistical unit mismatch:** repeated perturbations of the same 90 base combinations are described as 2,610 independent cases and analyzed with an i.i.d. Wilson interval.
4. **Core claim not directly tested:** no explicit direct fact-write bypass attempt or systematic enumeration of every fact-writing path.
5. **No human or field evidence:** the paper cannot support usability, reviewer benefit, automation-bias mitigation, or production effectiveness.
