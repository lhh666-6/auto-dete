## Principal remaining risks

1. **External validity:** all reported evaluation data are synthetic. Data sensitivity explains why production records are not public, but it does not substitute for real-data validation.
2. **Application completeness:** complete-form success is 60%; blur and rotation reveal QR/alignment brittleness.
3. **Human factors:** programmatic confirmation verifies the software path but does not establish reviewer speed, error detection, workload, or susceptibility to misleading suggestions.
4. **Novelty:** reviewers may view candidate--fact separation as a disciplined integration of existing selective prediction, approval, provenance, and capability ideas rather than a sufficiently new expert-system method.
5. **Security scope:** the executable boundary tests are valuable but intentionally narrower than a penetration test or access-control proof.
