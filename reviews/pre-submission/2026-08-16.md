## Generative-AI image policy

Elsevier's journal policy was updated in June 2026. It does **not** impose a blanket ban on every AI-assisted image: explanatory diagrams may be permitted with per-caption and manuscript disclosure; data visualizations must be directly and reproducibly derived from underlying data; primary research images may not be generated or altered; and general-purpose generative AI must not be used for graphical abstracts. Because journal pages and cached guidance can lag behind policy revisions, the present ESWA package takes the lowest-risk route: no generative-AI-created or altered image is included. Diagrams are author-controlled TikZ vectors, and quantitative plots are programmatically generated from hashed artifacts.

Official policy: https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals
