Statements and Declarations

Data and software availability
Entry point. The study rests on constructed tasks and archived review checkpoints. The project repository (https://github.com/lhh666-6/auto-dete) carries a fixed release (tag ase-layout-v13-2026-10-24) whose reproduction guide (https://github.com/lhh666-6/auto-dete/blob/ase-layout-v13-2026-10-24/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) orders the offline workflow and names every archive below.

Frozen versions. The controlled experiments are held in the frozen controlled-experiment archive (https://github.com/lhh666-6/auto-dete/tree/2645e5e18c900ea91c9c980e44195dc71e410432/DKE-supplement); the reference implementation, formal models and earlier historical evidence in the reference implementation and formal models (https://github.com/lhh666-6/auto-dete/tree/c6d512843c905cab6d8521dd8c914f7fb26d85ae/latest); and the complete original ledger, responses, states, scorer, tables, figure sources and hashes in the complete original ledger (https://github.com/lhh666-6/auto-dete/tree/475e66f549613aaaaa62c2ec81ffdee483636c54/cloud-handoff) (Online Resource 1). Supplementary A retains its separate freeze, complete attempts, independent scores and source hashes, with the offline entry point in S12. Original and supplementary ledgers remain separate.

Archive contents. Online Resource 3 bundles the editable sources and seven reproduction archives, whose manifests record their files and SHA-256 digests. The X1/X2 archive holds versioned sources, two saved executions and independent offline SQLite verification of native approval and binding scope (Online Resource 2, A11). The native-contract archive holds the scripted native-to-store freeze, unchanged dependencies, two saved runs, an independent SQL scorer, negative controls and no hosted model call (Online Resource 2, A14). The three native-{SDK} archives hold frozen protocols, code, cases, seals, raw per-cell effects, scores and portable offline verifiers: correction-consumption (504 cells), Agno entrypoint (54) and reviewed-instance substitution (16) (Online Resource 2, A17). The feedback-sensitivity archive holds the frozen exploratory study, raw trajectories and read-only instructions in REPRODUCE.md; the dynamic-recovery archive holds the pilot protocol, eight hosted trajectories, sixteen rule controls, independent scoring and offline replay.

Reanalysis and new runs. In Online Resource 3, five standard-library scripts recompute the recovery-cost readback and hashes, both archived examples, availability for all 32 G pairs, the scientific statistics and feedback strata, and Table \ref{tab:recovery-decomposition}; REPRODUCE-RECOVERY.md lists the two external archive dependencies, their pinned URLs, digests and offline commands, and -{-output} writes derived files separately. These analyses add no runs. Reanalysis needs no model calls; new hosted runs require provider access.

Ethics approval and consent to participate
The prospective experiment uses constructed tasks and a deterministic host reviewer, with no new human participants. In the historical annotation study, one author (X.W.) and one non-author volunteer independently coded benign completion outcomes; L.H. adjudicated their single inter-coder disagreement and separately reviewed eleven cases in which the strict trajectory label and the endpoint rule disagreed; a separate textual-acknowledgment audit with 39 human--rule disagreements was not adjudicated and is reported only as human--rule agreement. X.W. also completed the textual-acknowledgment annotation. Only pseudonymous annotator identifiers were collected. Both coders were informed of the task and data use, and the non-author coder participated voluntarily. The authors assessed the annotation of generated outputs as not requiring ethics approval. Online Resource 1, S5.2 details the annotation procedure. No animal subjects were involved.
