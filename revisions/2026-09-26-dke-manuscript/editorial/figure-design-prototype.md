# Conceptual image-generation prototype — 27 September 2026

Tool: OpenAI image-generation tool accessed through Codex. The tool did not expose a model or version identifier. OpenAI Codex (GPT-6) specified the scientific objects and inspected the generated result. This prototype is retained for editorial traceability and is **not** a manuscript figure or Graphical Abstract.

Style reference: the author's supplied infographic, with navy headings, a white background, pale-blue panels, blue/orange comparisons, document icons, and directional arrows. No scientific claim or data was taken from that reference.

Design brief: four panels explaining (a) two distinct candidates with proposal 100 and final corrected value 101, (b) exact candidate-bound human authorization while retaining proposal 100, (c) atomic admission with freshness and binding checks, and (d) the changed quantity source and unchanged batch source. The numbers are an explanatory construction, not an experimental observation.

Inspection found that the raster prototype labels a field source with an authorization identifier or a predecessor record identifier, instead of the required source-transition identifier. It also presents proposal retention as a downstream step after authorization. These visual relations are unsuitable as the paper's formal representation. The final vector diagrams keep the visual hierarchy while correcting those relationships; their script, PDF/SVG exports, figure captions, and source hashes are included.

Prototype: `figures/concepts/ai-admission-design-prototype.png`.

The final Figure 2 reads E1/E2 counts directly from manuscript result tables and the E3 timed count from CSV summaries. None of the result values is painted by an image model. Storage footprint is displayed as a separate measurement, rather than being counted among the 22,400 timed calls.
